My Year 2 Web Design Project

A closer look at a website I designed during my second year at the University of Toronto, exploring web design, creativity, and user experience.

01 / Project Overview

During my second year at the University of Toronto, I worked on a web design project that allowed me to explore how websites are structured and designed. The experience helped me develop my understanding of web development and visual design.

02 / Challenges & Solutions

One of the challenges in developing a website is finding the right balance between visual appearance and usability. This project gave me the opportunity to think about page layout, navigation, and how visitors interact with a website.

03 / Results & What I Learned

Through this project, I gained more experience with web design and learned the importance of creating a clear, organized, and user-friendly website. It also encouraged me to continue developing my creative and technical skills.
